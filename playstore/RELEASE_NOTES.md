## What's New in Version 1.0.0

- Initial Public Production Release of Apex Equities.
- Real-Time GSE Live Floor Feeds: Instant prices and volume streaming from the Ghana Stock Exchange floor.
- 100% Authentic Corporate Logos: Inlined vector and high-resolution brand assets for all 40+ GSE and global equities.
- Apex AI Research Assistant: Next-generation financial intelligence powered by Google Gemini AI with local GSE tax and regulatory grounding.
- Apex Axis 5-Factor Valuation Radar: Deterministic institutional health scores (Value, Dividend, Health, Past, Future).
- Interactive Portfolio Tracker: Holding-level P&L, asset allocation, and Ghana Tax Certificate generation.
- 7 Institutional Financial Calculators: Compound interest, T-Bills vs. stocks comparator, broker fees schedule, inflation Fisher adjustments, and DCA calculator.
- Offline First: Fast local caching with full offline support.
